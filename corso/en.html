Do my customers see who processes the payment?

No. Your customers only see your name, your logo and your colors. The payment infrastructure behind the scenes is completely invisible — it's your checkout, your brand, period. Zero third-party logos on the buyer side.

Which niches are accepted?

All legal niches. Fashion, beauty, tech, dropshipping, print-on-demand, supplements, training — we have no industry restrictions. If it's legal, we collect.

When do I get my money?

Instantly. As soon as a customer confirms their order, the money is transferred straight to your merchant balance. No 7, 14 or 30-day wait — payment is real-time.

How does the install work?

Fast, with no code to write. You connect your Shopify store in the dashboard, configure your account, and it's live. Your customers see your checkout on the next order.

Is it compatible with Shopify?

Yes, Shopify is our main platform. It also works on WooCommerce and Prestashop. The checkout automatically adapts to your colors, your logo, and your store name.

Is it secure?

Fully. All data is AES-256 encrypted. We never store any card data — everything routes through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified infrastructure. EU hosting, GDPR compliant.

How does the commission work?

You only pay on actual sales. Each month we tally your sales and generate an automatic invoice. No subscription, no fixed fees — we earn if you earn.

Can I manage multiple stores?

Yes. A single dashboard for all your stores. Each with its own settings, its own checkout, its own tracking. Ideal for multi-store operators.

What's the difference with Stripe?

Stripe rejects certain niches (dropshipping, supplements, CBD) and holds your funds for 7 days. Corsopay accepts all legal niches, you get paid instantly, and your checkout is 100% your brand — no third-party logos.

Does it work for dropshipping?

It's literally built for that. High-risk niches accepted, instant payouts to reinvest in ads immediately, and a pro checkout that reassures your customers. No more stressing over a Stripe refusal.

How does Meta / TikTok tracking work?

Paste your Pixel ID into the dashboard, that's it. We automatically send Purchase events server-side (Conversions API) after every captured payment. Reliable tracking, even with ad blockers.

Can I customize the checkout colors?

Yes, everything: primary color, logo, displayed name, and even the full theme (light/dark). Your checkout looks like your site, not a generic PayPal page.

How does Corsopay handle service quality and chargebacks?

We continuously monitor the health of every merchant account to protect the payment ecosystem. Merchants who fulfill their orders properly never have to worry about anything, payouts stay instant. In case of a high risk profile (chargeback rate above 1%, unfulfilled deliveries, repeated disputes), a temporary reserve may be applied on payouts to cover potential refunds, in line with standard practices across the payment industry. Full terms are detailed in our Terms of Service section 4 (Reserve fund and security deposit).
