Terms of Service

Last updated: 2026

1. Purpose of Services

These Terms of Service govern access to and use of the StripeVault platform, specialized in introducing buyers and sellers of verified, aged Stripe and Shopify Payments accounts for e-commerce operations.

2. Verification of Listing Metrics

StripeVault audits key listing metadata published on the marketplace (`Buy`), including:

  • Creation Date & Account Seniority
  • Gross Processed Sales History
  • Payout Speed (Instant Payout 0m or T+1 24h Daily)
  • Complete Verification Packages (Selfie KYC identity, corporate registry docs, supplier purchase invoices, and stock proof).

3. Escrow Terms & Settlement

To protect both parties, transaction funds are placed into neutral third-party escrow until the buyer confirms receipt, verifies admin credentials, and tests the Anti-Detect browser profile.

4. User Responsibilities

Buyers agree to adhere to recommended operating security protocols (maintaining the Anti-Detect browser session, using clean dedicated IPs, and keeping dispute rates under 0.5%) during ongoing account operations.
