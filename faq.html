Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about purchasing and operating verified Stripe and Shopify Payments accounts, creation age, payout speeds, Selfie KYC & stock packages, and escrow protection.

1. What verifications are pre-approved on the accounts?

Every Stripe and Shopify Payments account is sold **100% verified**. The package includes:

• Selfie & KYC Identity: High-definition ID scans and selfie identity of the original account representative.

• Corporate Registration: Official company registration docs (KBIS for France, UK Companies House for UK LLCs, or US EIN for US LLCs).

• Stock Proof & Invoices: Real supplier purchase invoices and 3PL fulfillment stock proof documents that have already been submitted and approved by the processor.

2. Why are Creation Date (Age) and Processed Volume important?

The **Creation Date** (e.g. 2021, 2022) establishes long-term account seniority. The **Processed Volume** (e.g. $50,000 to $300,000+) proves the account has processed numerous real transactions with a clean payment history, bypassing automated risk holds common on fresh un-warmed accounts.

3. What is the difference between Instant Payout and T+1 (24h Daily) payouts?

• Instant Payout (0-minute on-demand): You can initiate a transfer on demand and receive funds to your linked bank account or debit card in less than 60 seconds.

• Payout T+1 (24h Daily): The processor automatically executes a daily bank payout on a rolling 24-hour schedule for sales processed the previous day.

4. Do you offer both Stripe AND Shopify Payments accounts?

Yes. Our inventory includes verified **Stripe** accounts (France, UK LLC, US LLC, Hong Kong Ltd) as well as **Shopify Payments** accounts (France, UK LLC), all delivered with full admin access and complete verification dossiers.

5. Do you supply the Anti-Detect browser profile and dedicated proxy?

Yes, 100%. Upon handover, we supply a pre-configured Anti-Detect browser profile (AdsPower, GoLogin, or Multilogin) pre-loaded with the original session cookies and a dedicated clean residential proxy matching the account's jurisdiction for seamless, flag-free operation.

6. How does third-party Escrow protect my funds during the deal?

Purchase funds are safely locked in a neutral third-party escrow account. The seller receives funds only after you inspect the account, verify admin credentials, creation date, processed volume, and confirm full handover.

7. What warranty and replacement guarantee is included?

Every account purchased on CorsoVault comes with a full 7-Day (1 Week) Replacement Warranty covering initial onboarding, login testing, and payout setup.
