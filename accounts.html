Risk Architecture

Why Fresh Accounts Fail vs Why Aged Accounts Scale

Understanding the automated risk scoring algorithms of payment processors.

❌ The Fresh Account Trap (0 Volume, 0 Age)

  • Velocity Trigger: A surge in daily sales (e.g. from $0 to $3,000/day during ad scaling) triggers instant automated account freezing.
  • Rolling Reserves: Processors routinely apply a 20% to 30% rolling reserve held for 90 to 180 days.
  • Instant Payout Disabled: New accounts must wait 7 to 14 days for their first settlement.
  • Secondary KYC Blocks: Requests for warehouse selfies, tracking numbers, and supplier invoices freeze operations.

✅ The CorsoVault Senior Asset (Aged + Pre-Warmed)

  • Established Risk Trust: The algorithm has already recognized thousands of successful clean transactions over 2 to 4 years.
  • No Rolling Reserve: 0% funds held in reserve; 100% of revenue flows straight to your bank account.
  • Instant Payout Activated: Withdraw funds to your bank card within minutes of sales.
  • Turnkey KYC Cleared: Passport, utility bill, Selfie KYC dossier, and company registration documents are already approved.

Frequently Asked Questions About Account Acquisition

How do I connect the account to my store without triggering a ban?

Every account is delivered with a pre-configured Anti-Detect browser profile (AdsPower / GoLogin) and matching residential proxy. You simply import the profile, connect your store via API keys or official Shopify/WooCommerce plugins, and process sales with zero identity disruption.

Can I change the payout bank account to my own bank?

Yes. You can attach your own Wise Business, Revolut Business, Mercury Bank, Relay, or local bank account in the matching currency (USD / EUR / GBP). We provide a step-by-step guideline on attaching new bank accounts safely without triggering automated security locks.

How does the 3rd-party Escrow protection work?

Your payment is held in neutral Escrow custody (Crypto USDT / Bank Wire) until you receive the archive, log in, verify credentials, and pass test transactions. The seller only receives payment after you confirm complete satisfaction.
