Frequently Asked Questions

Common Questions Before Purchasing an Account

What verifications are pre-approved on the accounts?

Every Stripe and Shopify Payments account is delivered **100% verified**. The package includes **Selfie KYC identity verification** of the original account representative, proof of address, company formation documents (KBIS, UK LLC, US EIN), and a complete dossier of **stock proof & supplier invoices** approved by the processor.

Do you offer both Stripe and Shopify Payments accounts?

Yes. We offer verified **Stripe** accounts and **Shopify Payments** accounts across multiple jurisdictions (France, UK LLC, US LLC, Hong Kong Ltd), all pre-warmed with real processed transaction volume.

How do Instant Payout and T+1 daily payouts work?

With **Instant Payout**, funds can be transferred to your bank account or card within minutes on demand. With **T+1 (Daily)** payouts, the processor automatically sends accumulated sales daily on a 24-hour rolling schedule.

How does the secure handover with Anti-Detect browser work?

Upon delivery, we provide an Anti-Detect browser profile (AdsPower, GoLogin, or Multilogin) pre-configured with original session cookies and a dedicated clean proxy. This ensures your first logins look 100% natural and safe to the processor's risk system.
