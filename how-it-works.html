Guarantee & Compliance

Why the "Verif Done" Pack (Selfie, KYC, Stock) Matters

Every account sold on CorsoVault comes with pre-approved verification documents to provide you with peace of mind during operation.

Included in the Verification Dossier

  • Selfie & KYC Identity Approved

    High-resolution ID scans and selfie identity of the original representative to satisfy any identity check requested by Stripe or Shopify Payments.

  • Legal Corporate Registration

    Official registration docs (KBIS for France, Companies House for UK LLCs, or EIN / Certificate of Formation for US LLCs).

  • Stock Proof & Supplier Invoices

    Real supplier purchase invoices and 3PL logistics stock proof documents that have already been submitted and approved by the payment processor.

3 Core Operational Guarantees Included

Every account delivery on CorsoVault is protected by our standard 3-tier operational coverage:

  • 1. Anti-Detect Browser Profile Supplied By Us

    We supply a fully pre-configured AdsPower or GoLogin browser profile with exact session cookies pre-loaded for 100% smooth login.

  • 2. Dedicated Clean Proxy Included

    Every account includes a dedicated clean residential proxy matching the account's original country and browser session history.

  • 3. Full 7-Day (1 Week) Replacement Warranty

    Every purchase includes a 7-day replacement warranty covering initial onboarding and handover verification.
